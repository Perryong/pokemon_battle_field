# Kanto party battles
Build a local team of one to six Kanto Pokémon, each with one to four compatible Gen I moves, and generate an equally sized rival party. Keep the original lead selections and legal moves when upgrading an existing save. The first slot leads; each slot has independent moves, even if the same species appears twice. Allow adding, editing, removing and promoting a member in the Pokédex. Applying team changes starts a fresh match; just browsing must leave the match alone.

Use the installed Gen I simulator's move, switch, forced switch and wait requests. Show which party members can be sent out and why other choices are unavailable. Replay the actual switches on the field. Rematch resets both parties; exhibition stays independent. The requested matching rival generator supersedes a manually assembled rival team: seed a diverse set of distinct species with legal loadouts, preserving the existing rival lead as an optional customization. No changes to the Gen I move catalogue.
