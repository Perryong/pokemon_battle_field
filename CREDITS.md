# Pokémon Battle Field credits

The character rigs, trainer miniatures, pitch, match rules, animation, and interface were made for this unofficial Pokémon fan scene. Pokémon names and likenesses belong to their respective owners.

| Packaged mesh | Credit (CC BY 4.0) | Changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| assets/grandstand.glb | [Grandstand Seating Run by Polyfork](https://polyfork.dev/asset/grandstand-seating-run-fc9023) | Original GLB bytes. Instanced and scaled to a miniature; rendered with the arena palette. |
| assets/floodlight.glb | [Floodlight Mast by Polyfork](https://polyfork.dev/asset/floodlight-mast-4482be) | Original GLB bytes. Scaled, oriented and palette-rendered. |

Both model pages and GLB asset metadata carry the [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) option. In-app credits are available from the footer.

PixelPost, the adapted palette, creature helper functions and screenshot harness are from [Diorama](https://github.com/sloemo01/diorama/tree/c790df5007be54cccb6c083f7743fae120c89cf4) by Nehal Kanak / Sloemo01, MIT (src/lib/LICENSE). Reference contact sheet in refs is derived from that repository's Pikachu vs. Charizard preview and is not used as a game asset. Three.js is MIT; the self-contained build carries both license notices.

The 151 Kanto bodies are original procedural miniatures. Their index portraits are rendered from the same meshes. No game sprites or third-party Pokémon models are used. National IDs, English names, default-form types, official listed dimensions, and move learnsets come from [PokéAPI's CSV data](https://github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi/tree/6ea97b5641faa12e090866e8144ae8f57764fc64/data/v2/csv). The species and character designs remain the property of their respective owners.

Generation I battle resolution uses @pkmn/sim, built from Pokémon Showdown, under the MIT license. Historical species and learnset data use @pkmn/dex. Move parameters come from the Pokémon Red disassembly. The move presentations are original effects designed for this stadium.

Suggested set names and move choices are from the [Smogon RBY Strategy Pokédex](https://www.smogon.com/dex/rb/pokemon/), via the [pinned @pkmn/smogon set data](https://github.com/pkmn/smogon/blob/86f575d661ab180b182e9e9b021fb550f4ade257/data/sets/gen1.json). Each imported set uses one legal choice per slot. Role descriptions are written for Pokémon Battle Field. Metapod and Kakuna use local Field Guide sets. The OU option uses the installed Showdown simulator and team validator. [Smogon RBY OU rules](https://www.smogon.com/dex/rb/formats/ou/)
