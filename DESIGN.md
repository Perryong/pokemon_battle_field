# Pokémon Battle Field • Circuit Final 01
Start in a playable Pikachu vs. Charizard match; a second mode runs a 45-second deterministic exhibition, with shared characters, battle rig and events. Home camera: three-quarter tabletop view; orbit, zoom and reset supported. The low-poly arena uses the reference's miniature slab, saturated pixel palette and a Poké Ball pitch. Locally bundled Polyfork grandstands and floodlights form the stadium architecture; authored jointed creatures and trainers occupy the pitch. The exhibition opens with a throw, replays turns from a seeded battle between the selected teams, and reports the result. Play uses Gen I turn order, PP, accuracy and damage, enemy retaliation, HP, win/loss and rematch. Desktop and phone must allow all moves and both modes.

## Kanto roster
The 151 base-form Generation I species have independent orbitable solids. The same model factory builds the stadium pair, the inspection view and UI portraits. Keep one local unit per metre and normalize each neutral body once; animate an outer transform so camera fit and action never change its baseline size. Body centerlines are measured explicitly for coiled models. The Pokédex shows either a height or a length and includes a neutral 1.7 m trainer for comparison. Each trainer can field a party of up to six. The first member leads, and the rival party matches the player’s party size. Each member keeps its own stats, moves and battle state. The match uses the RBY simulator for moves, damage, status, type matchups and turn order.

## Character motion
Each species binds its own armature to the neutral sculpture: joints carry existing mesh pieces about anatomical pivots. Pose sampling is an absolute function of idle time, action kind, wind-up, strike and impact; a seek or interrupted turn never accumulates rotations. Neutral geometry alone provides the Pokédex measurements and camera fit. Player and rival use the same sampler but only the actor attacks. Contact flags determine travel; the defender reacts at the impact beat. The inspector previews the same clips and stops its animation loop when closed.
